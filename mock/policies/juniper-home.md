# Juniper Home — Returns, Price Adjustment and Warranty Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026. Juniper Home is a fictional store used for the Snapback demo.

## 1. Returns

Return any item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Use our online returns portal with your order number and shipping ZIP code, or contact help@juniper-home.example.

Items should be clean and complete. Used small appliances may be returned only if defective.

## 2. Return shipping and restocking fees

Juniper Home never charges a restocking fee.

Defective or damaged items: we provide a free prepaid return label.

Non-defective returns: a $7.95 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## 3. Price adjustments

If an item you bought goes down in price at Juniper Home within 14 days of delivery, we will refund the difference. Request a price adjustment through the returns portal. Limit one adjustment per item. Flash-sale and bundle prices are excluded.

## 4. Warranty

Small kitchen appliances (blenders, kettles, toasters) include a 2-year limited warranty from delivery covering defects in materials and workmanship. Textiles and cookware include a 1-year warranty. To file a claim, provide your order number and a short description or photo of the defect.
