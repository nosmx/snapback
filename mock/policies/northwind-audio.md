# Northwind Audio — Returns, Price Adjustment and Warranty Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026. Northwind Audio is a fictional store used for the Snapback demo.

## 1. Returns

You may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a refund to the original payment method. Start a return at our returns portal with your order number and shipping ZIP code. No account login is required.

Items must include all original accessories. Returns are inspected within 3 business days of receipt.

## 2. Return shipping and restocking fees

Defective or damaged items: return shipping is free and no restocking fee applies.

Non-defective items that have been opened: a 15% restocking fee is deducted from the refund. Return shipping for non-defective items is free.

## 3. Price adjustments

If the price of an item you bought drops on northwind-audio.example within 14 days of delivery, we will refund the difference once per item. Request a price adjustment through the returns portal or by contacting support. Clearance and open-box prices are excluded.

## 4. Warranty

All Northwind Audio products carry a 1-year limited warranty from the delivery date that covers defects in materials and workmanship. Warranty claims are handled as repair or replacement at our discretion. Physical damage, liquid damage and normal wear are not covered.

## 5. Damaged batteries

Items with a damaged, swollen, punctured or exposed lithium battery must not be sent in standard return mail. Return them one of two ways:

- In-store drop-off at any Northwind Audio store. Staff seal the item in a fire-safe bag, and the refund is issued at drop-off.
- A prepaid battery-safe return label (UN3481), requested through the returns portal. The carrier collects the item in a battery-safe pouch within 5 business days, and the refund is issued after inspection.

Defective-item terms (section 2) still apply: no restocking fee and no return shipping charge.
