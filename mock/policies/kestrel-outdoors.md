# Kestrel Outdoors — Returns, Pricing and Guarantee Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2026. Kestrel Outdoors is a fictional store used for the Snapback demo.

## 1. Returns

Unworn, unwashed items with original tags may be returned within 45 days of delivery for a refund to the original payment method. Start a return at our returns portal with your order number and shipping ZIP code.

Tents and packs may be returned within 45 days if they have not been used outdoors.

## 2. Return shipping and restocking fees

Kestrel Outdoors does not charge restocking fees.

Defective items: return shipping is free.

Non-defective returns: a $6.95 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## 3. Price adjustments

Kestrel Outdoors does not offer price adjustments or price matching. If a price drops after your purchase, you may return the original item within the return window and place a new order at the current price.

## 4. Lifetime guarantee

Kestrel Outdoors gear is guaranteed against defects in workmanship for the life of the product. Normal wear and tear, accidents and alterations are not covered. File a guarantee claim through the returns portal with your order number and a photo of the defect.
