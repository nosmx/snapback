# Northwind Audio — Headphone and Earbud Review Summaries

Summaries of verified-buyer reviews, compiled November 2026. Northwind Audio is a fictional store used for the Snapback demo.

## Arc ANC Wireless Headphones (NWA-ARC-ANC)

Average rating 3.1 from 842 reviews. Sound and noise cancelling are praised, but 23% of reviews report the plastic hinge cracking within three months, usually at the left ear-cup joint. Several reviewers say replacements failed the same way.

## Arc ANC 2 Wireless Headphones (NWA-ARC-ANC2)

Average rating 4.6 from 1,210 reviews. The second generation replaced the plastic hinge with a reinforced metal one; hinge failures dropped below 1% of reviews. Battery life (about 40 hours) and comfort are frequently praised.

## Arc ANC Pro Headphones (NWA-ARC-ANC-PRO)

Average rating 4.7 from 503 reviews. Reviewers like the lossless wired mode and adaptive noise cancelling; the main complaint is the price.

## Halo Over-Ear Headphones (NWA-HALO-OE)

Average rating 4.1 from 377 reviews. Light and comfortable, with average noise cancelling. A few reports of ear-cushion wear after a year.

## Studio Monitor Headphones (NWA-STUDIO-MH)

Average rating 4.4 from 655 reviews. Wired, closed-back monitors; praised for accuracy, not intended for commuting.

## Pulse True Wireless Earbuds (NWA-PULSE-EB)

Average rating 3.2 from 931 reviews. Sound and noise cancelling are good for the price, but 18% of reviews report the housing cracking open near the stem within two months, and several mention the battery being exposed or swelling afterwards.

## Pulse Pro Wireless Earbuds (NWA-PULSE-PRO)

Average rating 4.7 from 1,064 reviews. The Pro uses a reinforced one-piece housing; housing failures appear in under 1% of reviews. Adaptive noise cancelling and the 9-hour battery are frequently praised.
